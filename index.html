What is EMFACE

Like going to the gym, for your face

EMFACE is the first non-invasive treatment that works on your skin and the muscles beneath it at the same time. Synchronized radiofrequency rebuilds collagen and elastin while HIFES energy gently tones the muscles that support your face, needle-free, with zero downtime.

01Radiofrequency

Heats the deeper layers to rebuild collagen and elastin, firming and smoothing skin.

02HIFES muscle stim

Thousands of gentle contractions tone and lift the muscles that support your face.
